# Product Brief — Relasy

## Product summary

Relasy is a lightweight CRM web application that helps small businesses keep customer information organized, track sales opportunities, record customer-related activity, and remember when to follow up.

Relasy is not a chat application. The initial release works without WhatsApp API integration or any unofficial WhatsApp connection.

## Problem

Small businesses often keep customer names, sales status, notes, and follow-up reminders across memory, chat history, spreadsheets, and paper notes. This makes it easy to forget a promising lead, miss a follow-up, or lose context about a customer.

## Intended initial audience

Small business owners and small sales-oriented teams who need a simpler alternative to a complex enterprise CRM. The first version should work for one business owner without requiring a large-team setup.

## Product promise

Know who your customers are, where each opportunity stands, what happened last, and what to do next.

## Product principles

1. Useful in the first session: the user can add a customer and record a follow-up without a lengthy setup.
2. Fast to scan and operate: common tasks take few steps.
3. Clear rather than clever: use familiar labels and predictable interactions.
4. Trustworthy: make saved state, validation, errors, and data ownership clear.
5. Focused: do the core CRM jobs well before adding integrations or advanced automation.
6. Natural Indonesian: interface language should sound like a real Indonesian product, not machine-translated software.

## Initial release success signals

These are product-quality goals, not claims about actual usage:
- A new user can create a customer record without assistance.
- A user can move a sales opportunity through its stages.
- A user can set, find, complete, and reschedule a follow-up.
- Customer activity is visible in context.
- Core workflows persist correctly across refreshes and re-login where authentication is implemented.
- No critical or high-severity defects remain in core workflows.
